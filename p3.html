Bong Go denies links to Discayas: “Wala akong kinalaman sa kanila”.

POLITICS / October 27, 2025

Go’s statement came after Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) Secretary Vince Dizon revealed that his office and the Office of the Ombudsman are investigating possible ties between the Discayas and CLTG Builders, a company in Davao City reportedly owned by Go’s father. The senator, however, rejected any insinuation of wrongdoing and maintained that he has nothing to hide. News, photos, or videos that arouse the interest of netizens would often go viral on social media, due to the attention netizens give them.
